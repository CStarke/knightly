Expectations 
Each team member will contribute consistently throughout each sprint and complete assigned tasks by the agreed-upon deadlines. 
Hospitable coding should be practiced by all team members. Code comments aren’t optional. 
Tasks should be started early enough to allow time for testing, review, and unexpected problems. 
Team members are expected to be punctual for scheduled meetings and communicate ahead of time if they will be late or absent. 
If sickness, interviews, academic commitments, work, or other unexpected circumstances interfere with a team member's responsibilities, they should inform the team as soon as possible. 
When necessary, the team will redistribute work or adjust internal deadlines to accommodate unexpected circumstances. 
We recognize that the project workload may increase toward the end of the semester and will plan accordingly. However, a grace period of a one-time warning within a sprint for delays shall be given, and after that, a report to the Professor shall be made and an appropriate evaluation by the Project Manager. 
Communication 
Microsoft Teams will be our primary communication platform. 
Team members should check Teams regularly and respond within [12/24 hours] on weekdays. 
Urgent issues, blockers, or expected delays should be communicated as soon as possible. 
Important project decisions should be communicated to the entire team rather than only between individual members.

Equitable Contribution & Conflict Resolution 
We will disagree with ideas, designs, and technical decisions without attacking or insulting the person proposing them. 
If a teamwork or contribution problem occurs: 
Communicate directly: Bring the concern respectfully to the teammate involved and explain the specific issue. 
Discuss as a team: If the problem continues, discuss it during a team meeting and agree on concrete expectations or corrective actions. 
Document the solution: Record important agreements, responsibilities, and deadlines so expectations are clear. [Keep everything in Teams] 
Follow up: At the next meeting, determine whether the agreed-upon changes have occurred. 
Contact the instructor: If the issue continues despite these steps, or seriously threatens the team's ability to complete the project, the team will involve the instructor. 
The goal of conflict resolution is to solve the problem fairly and maintain a productive working relationship.
